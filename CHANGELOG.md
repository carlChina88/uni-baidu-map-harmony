## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-24

### Added

- Initial public adapter for uni-app HarmonyOS native map embedding.
- Native `baidu-map` embed registration.
- Lazy Baidu SDK initialization with explicit privacy-consent input.
- BD09 center synchronization and debounced center-change events.
- Optional marker and PopView support.
- Bilingual documentation, contribution policy, security policy, licensing,
  and source validation script.

### Deliberately omitted

- The vendor Baidu HAR binary.
- Host-app assets, APIs, pages, business state, and location permissions.
